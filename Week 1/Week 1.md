##  Approach
I made an arcade based game where you collect souls from the sky and avoid enemys like skulls and bats. Its a simple catcher game that has an 8-bit design to it.

Following the requirements of an api I added a real time weather system that effects the game based on the weather on the day. It also has 5 different preselected weather options you can choose from. The weather affects the gameplay. Along side this the game has a normal and hard mode for increased spawn rates for a harder gameplay experience.

---

## Result

The result of the game was exactly what I wanted. The weather system works great and the game runs smoothly. I added buffs that you can collect like a soul magnet and shield this helps the gameplay become more randomised.

I did however have issues with the sound. Certain sounds were glitching when implemented, alongside this the music also lost quality so i had to go through the wav files and make sure everything was implemented correctly. Being able to mute the sound allowed me to realise the backing music wasnt the issue it was the quality of the sfx.
